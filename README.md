# Euphoria

## About the Series

**Euphoria** is an American teen drama on *HBO*, created by **Sam Levinson**. It is based on an Israeli series of the same name and first aired on **June 16, 2019**.

--

## Why It's My Favourite

> A raw, stylish look at growing up, addiction, identity, and love.

I like it because of:

- The bold, colorful **cinematography** and makeup
- The emotional, honest **storytelling**
- The powerful **soundtrack** and score by Labrinth
- The ~~boring~~ unforgettable characters
